Suggested conversion flow

A roofer site usually performs best when the user can understand the offer fast, confirm the company is legitimate, and take one next step without friction.

1. Immediate problem statement

Lead with what the business does, who it helps, and the fastest way to get an estimate.

2. Service and coverage clarity

Break down repair, replacement, and storm-related work so visitors can self-identify quickly.

3. Real proof points later

Replace placeholders with real photos, team information, licensing details, and actual reviews when available.
