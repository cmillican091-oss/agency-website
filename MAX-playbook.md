# MAX — Clearwater Digital Outreach Playbook

> "Max" is the name for Clearwater Digital's outreach process and assistant.
> Target niches: **Home Services (roofers, HVAC, plumbers, contractors)** and **Dental practices**.

---

## 1. Who to call (qualifying a good prospect)

A great prospect usually has at least ONE of these:
- No website at all, or only a Facebook page
- A website that is broken or ugly on mobile phones
- A slow site, or one that clearly looks 10+ years old
- No way to book / request a quote online

How to find them (legitimately):
- Search Google Maps / Google for "[city] roofer", "[city] dentist", etc.
- Note businesses with weak or missing websites
- Use public business directories and their own listed contact info
- Do NOT buy scraped contact lists or harvest personal data — it creates legal risk (TCPA, spam laws) and converts worse anyway

Keep a simple spreadsheet: Business name | Phone | Current site (yes/no/bad) | Notes | Status

---

## 2. The cold-call script

**Opener**
"Hi, is this [Name]? Great — my name is [You], I run a small web studio called Clearwater Digital here in [area]. I'll be quick. I came across [Business] and noticed your website [doesn't show up well on phones / looks like it hasn't been updated in a while / I couldn't find one]. Mind if I ask you one quick question?"

**The hook (pick based on niche)**
- Home services: "Most folks looking for a roofer today pull out their phone and call whoever looks most trustworthy first. Is your current site bringing you calls, or is it mostly word of mouth right now?"
- Dental: "These days most new patients check out a practice online before booking. Are you happy with how your website looks to a new patient right now?"

**The pitch**
"That's exactly what I help with. I build clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites for [roofers / dental practices] that turn visitors into calls and bookings. I could put together a quick mockup of what yours could look like — no cost, no obligation. If you like it, we talk. If not, no harm done. Worth a look?"

**Close**
"Awesome. What's the best email to send the mockup to? And what's a good time later this week for a quick 10-minute call to walk through it?"

---

## 3. Objection handling

- "I already have a website." -> "Totally fair — a lot of the businesses I help did too. Mind if I take a quick look? If it's already working great on mobile and bringing you leads, I'll tell you honestly and leave you alone."
- "I'm too busy / not interested." -> "Completely understand, I'll be quick. Can I just email you a free sample mockup? Look at it whenever you have a minute, and only reply if you like it."
- "How much does it cost?" -> "Most of my projects start around [YOUR PRICE]. But before we talk money, let me show you what I'd actually build — then you can decide if it's worth it."
- "Can you send info?" -> "Definitely. What's the best email? I'll send a short overview plus a sample of what your site could look like."

## 5. Compliance reminders (important)

- Respect the National Do Not Call Registry for any personal/residential numbers.
- Identify yourself and your business honestly on every call.
- Keep an opt-out / don't-call-again list and honor it immediately.
- Never use fake reviews, fake clients, or fabricated testimonials.
- Call business numbers during reasonable business hours.
